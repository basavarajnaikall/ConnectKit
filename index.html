02 FIRST, THE IMPORTANT BIT

One OAuth.
Two different apps.

The provider’s OAuth app is your integration’s identity at a platform like Google or Slack. You create it, configure the redirect URL and permissions, then pass its client ID and secret to your integration.

Your users authorize your app to access their accounts. They never need to create an OAuth app or share their password with you.
